# Namma Bengaluru Buddy

**Your first 30 days in Bengaluru, sorted.** A rule-based agent for people moving to the city for work. It builds a personalised Week 1–4 plan, ranks neighbourhoods by commute, rent and monsoon risk, checks whether a quoted rent is fair, and flags risky clauses in a rental agreement, showing how much money is at stake.

Theme: **Smarter Streets, Better Living** (commute planning, neighbourhood flood and water risk).

## The problem

About 44 lakh of Bengaluru's 85 lakh residents (BBMP area, Census 2011) were born elsewhere. About 17%, roughly 14.5 lakh people, came from other states. Newcomers arrive with a joining date and little else. They don't know:
- which areas flood or depend on water tankers
- what rent is fair
- which agreement clauses are normal
- what order to do the paperwork in (agreement → Aadhaar → bank → gas → vehicle)

The information exists, but it's scattered across Reddit threads, brokers and word of mouth.

## Who it's for

Working professionals relocating to Bengaluru, especially from outside Karnataka, in their first month.

## Features

| | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Intake** | Arrival and joining dates, office, budget, household, vehicle, home state, diet, languages |
| **Snapshot card** | One screen showing tasks planned, top area plus 9 AM commute, ₹ at risk, red flags |
| **Week 1–4 plan** | Builds a dependency graph, topologically sorts it and schedules each task. Paperwork is capped in the joining week, and ticking a task off re-plans. |
| **Agentic re-plan** | "Simulate: agreement delayed by a week" re-runs the planner, highlights which tasks moved and explains each move in the trace |
| **Area finder** | Commute 40% + rent fit 40% + owner-rule fit 20%, minus a monsoon/civic risk penalty (flooding, water source, power cuts). Each card shows the 9 AM vs 2 PM commute and a suggested mode (bike, metro + walk, BMTC). |
| **Fair-rent check** | Median and range from listings. Falls back from locality to BHK ratio to nearby areas to zone, and shows a 0–100 confidence score. |
| **Agreement checker** | Regex and numeric rules (lock-in, one-sided termination, deposit, escalation, painting deduction and more). Shows a risk score, **money at risk in ₹**, and a ready-to-send negotiation message. |
| **Agent trace** | Every step explains why: which tasks were skipped or moved, how each score was computed, which clause triggered which rule |
| **Languages** | English / ಕನ್ನಡ / हिन्दी for headings, buttons and notes |

## Measurable impact

On the sample agreement, Buddy finds **₹2,50,200 at risk**:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Excess deposit: ₹3,00,000 − 3 × ₹30,000 | ₹2,10,000 |
| Extra year-2 rent at 12% vs the 5% norm: 7% × ₹30,000 × 12 | ₹25,200 |
| Fixed painting deduction | ₹15,000 |

It also produces a 21-task plan that fits in four weeks.

## Privacy and human review

- Everything runs in the browser, with no server, no API calls and no uploads. Agreement text never leaves the device.
- Task progress and language choice are stored only in this browser's localStorage.
- Buddy gives guidance, not legal advice. Before signing, the app tells users to have a lawyer or free legal aid from the Karnataka State Legal Services Authority (KSLSA) review the agreement.

## How to run

Open `index.html` in any browser. There's no build step and no install, and it works offline from `file://`. Click **⚡ Load demo profile → Generate My Plan**, then **📄 Load sample agreement → Scan**.

## Architecture

```
data/*.js      → window.DATA_* globals (tasks, areas + risk layer, rent listings, red-flag rules)
modules/*.js   → pure-ish engines: plan.js, areas.js, rent.js, redflags.js, i18n.js
app.js         → orchestrator: intake → areas → plan → rent pre-fill → snapshot card
```

The design principle is **AI at authoring time, rules at runtime**:
- The data and rules were drafted with AI and reviewed by a person.
- At runtime every answer is deterministic, explainable in the trace, and free to run.

## Limitations

- Rent, commute, flooding, water-source and power-cut data are **indicative**. They're compiled from public reporting and sample listings, not live feeds. Verify everything on a visit.
- Peak and off-peak commute times are derived from one typical figure using fixed multipliers.
- The agreement checker matches patterns. It can miss clauses with unusual wording and doesn't read PDFs.
